## What should you do as soon as lava appears?

Get off the floor by moving to the closest elevation you can reach cleanly. A recorded match shows LAVA turning the floor into a hazard and prompting players to climb. Look for the first reachable step before aiming for a higher platform, especially if you are starting in the open.

Find the route from your feet to the raised surface. A tall object across the arena looks promising from a distance, but you need to know how to get there and how to get up. A smaller nearby surface can be the better first move if it gets you away from the floor sooner.

| Where you are when the cue appears | What to look for first | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In open ground | A raised surface with a direct approach | Running toward a tall object before spotting its entrance |
| Beside an obstacle | A visible foothold or a clear route around it | Repeating a jump against the same blocked side |
| Already above the floor | A safe place to stand and a second route | Dropping into an area you cannot see |

## How can you tell whether a climb is reachable?

Trace the approach, first jump, and landing before committing. If you can see all three, start moving. If a platform has a clear landing but you cannot tell how to get onto it, look for a closer option that lets you keep moving while you reassess the arena.

Try this before the warning in a practice match. Pick one surface, then imagine reaching it from two different sides. A platform that is easy from one side can be a dead end from the other. This exercise makes it easier to pick a route under pressure without needing to memorize a particular arena layout.

## How do you stay safe after climbing?

Once you are above the floor, look at your landing spot and scan for an exit you can use from there. Read the next word before you move again. If the new hazard changes your route, compare the available landings and move only when you can see where you will end up.

| What changes next | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A threat approaches your platform | Whether you can move across the platform or step onto another raised surface |
| Players crowd your planned landing | Whether a different landing gives you more room to adjust |
| The floor looks clear again | Whether your actual landing and the path beyond it are safe |
