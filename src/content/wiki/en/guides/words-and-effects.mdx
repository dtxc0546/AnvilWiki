## Which word effects have been documented?

A [recorded match walkthrough](https://games.resuhive.com/survive-your-ideas/guide) documents two useful examples. LAVA changed the ground into a hazard, while PYRAMIDSOFGEZA created a structure players could approach. The examples show why the prompt matters: one changes where you can stand, and the other gives you a new destination.

| Input seen in the recording | Arena change | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LAVA | The floor became hazardous | A nearby climb with a landing you can see |
| PYRAMIDSOFGEZA | A pyramid rose from the sand | A route to the structure and the instruction on screen |

## How should you pick a word for practice?

Pick one word with an effect you can recognize, and check the arena before you submit it. If you try an example from this page, watch for the on-screen cue and compare the result with the recording. Keep your escape route in mind while you observe.

| Practice goal | What to observe |
| --- | --- |
| Recognize a floor hazard | Where the warning appears and which elevation you can reach |
| React to a new structure | How its appearance changes the routes around you |
| Learn an unfamiliar word | The exact input, the displayed cue, and the visible result |

## How do you react when a word changes the arena?

Read the prompt, identify what changed around your current position, and pick a destination you can reach. The best route depends on where you are when the word appears and what is already in the arena. Look for a clear landing or an exit before you commit.
