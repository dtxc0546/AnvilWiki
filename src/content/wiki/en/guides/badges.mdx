## Which badges can you aim for in your first matches?

Aim for My First Idea once you get a writing turn, then work toward Champion by finishing a match as the last player alive. These goals teach different parts of the game. The first gets you familiar with the writer prompt, while the second rewards surviving the whole match.

| Badge | Official requirement in plain English |
| --- | --- |
| [My First Idea](https://www.roblox.com/badges/1019581166207130) | Write your first word in a match |
| [Champion](https://www.roblox.com/badges/2496918138332016) | Win a match as the last player standing |
| [Deadly Idea](https://www.roblox.com/badges/2823463248112968) | Eliminate three players with one word |

If you have yet to take a writing turn, the [beginner guide](/guides/getting-started/) explains how to spot the prompt. The [word effects guide](/guides/words-and-effects/) gives two documented examples to help you recognize what changes after a word is entered.

## Which badges require a streak or longer progress?

Choose the badge whose condition fits what you want to practice. Survivor tracks a single long match, while Unstoppable asks for consecutive wins. Collector tracks discoveries in the Word Diary and Veteran tracks matches played, so they give you longer goals to work on between attempts at the match challenges.

| Badge | Official requirement in plain English |
| --- | --- |
| [Survivor](https://www.roblox.com/badges/356876462278508) | Survive 60 words in a single match |
| [Unstoppable](https://www.roblox.com/badges/3872117496334139) | Win two matches in a row |
| [Collector](https://www.roblox.com/badges/3136480215890615) | Discover 150 words in your Word Diary |
| [Veteran](https://www.roblox.com/badges/2734601290169684) | Play 100 matches |

For Survivor, practice looking for a new exit each time a word changes the arena. The [hazard guide](/guides/idea-and-hazard-tips/) walks through that decision.

## How were the requirements checked?

The conditions in these tables come from the public Roblox badge descriptions reviewed on November 8, 2026. Each badge name links to its own record. Use those records to confirm the requirement you are targeting, especially whether it applies to one match, consecutive wins, or progress tracked across matches.
