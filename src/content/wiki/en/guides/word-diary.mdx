## How do you earn the Collector badge?

The [official Collector badge](https://www.roblox.com/badges/3136480215890615) requires 150 words discovered in your Word Diary. Use the Diary as your record: compare its visible entries before and after a word appears. Keep a short list of ideas you still want to try on future writing turns.

| Goal | Where to check |
| --- | --- |
| Find out whether you already discovered a word | Look for its entry in the Word Diary. |
| See whether an attempt added a discovery | Compare the entries or count before and after, if shown. |
| Confirm the badge requirement | Read the Collector badge description on Roblox. |

## How can you use a writing turn to check a discovery?

Choose a word that you did not see in your Diary. Enter it when the game selects you as writer, and watch what appears in the arena. When you have a safe moment, check the Diary again. Note the exact spelling you entered and the entry the game recorded, especially if the names differ.

If a word creates a familiar effect, keep those two observations separate. You saw something happen in the arena, and you still need to check whether the Diary added an entry. The [words and effects guide](/guides/words-and-effects/) gives documented examples you can use to get oriented.

| What you observe | What you can record |
| --- | --- |
| A new named entry appears | Record the entry the Diary shows. |
| You see an effect but find no new entry | Record the effect and leave discovery credit open. |
| Another player introduces a word you wanted to try | Check your own Diary afterward to see whether it changed. |

## How is Collector different from the other word goals?

The [badge overview](/guides/badges/) lists My First Idea as a first writing goal and Collector as a discovery goal. If you are trying to unlock a stronger version of a specific word, see [Word Mastery](/guides/word-mastery/). Read the goal you are working toward before you choose what to try next.

**Source reviewed November 9, 2026.** The Roblox badge confirms the total of 150. The current menu location, duplicate credit and discoveries triggered by other players remain unverified by this wiki.
