## How do you unlock Unstoppable?

Win two consecutive matches for [Unstoppable](https://www.roblox.com/badges/3872117496334139). Start by aiming to finish one match as the last player alive, then use the same survival routine in your next match. Check the result after each finish so you know whether you are still on a streak.

| Badge | Official condition |
| --- | --- |
| [Champion](https://www.roblox.com/badges/2496918138332016) | Win a match as the last player standing. |
| [Unstoppable](https://www.roblox.com/badges/3872117496334139) | Win two matches in a row. |
| [Survivor](https://www.roblox.com/badges/356876462278508) | Survive 60 words in one match. |

## What should you do when only a few players are left?

Look at the route ahead and decide where you would go if your current spot stopped being safe. Pick a nearby fallback you can actually reach. A position that survives the current threat can still be hard to leave if another idea changes the path.

| Arena situation | Survival decision |
| --- | --- |
| Two routes are open | Remember the shorter fallback before taking the first path. |
| Your climb ends at a dead end | Look for a way down or across before committing to it. |
| You get a writing turn late in the match | Think through where you will move after the effect appears. |
| The last opponent is far away | Keep watching nearby hazards while waiting for the match to resolve. |

For routes that change as hazards stack up, use the [hazard guide](/guides/idea-and-hazard-tips/). If lava is the immediate problem, read the [lava guide](/guides/how-to-survive-lava/) for advice on picking a climb and a backup.

## How should you handle the match after your first win?

Start the next match by reassessing the arena. Notice which route is open now and which nearby spot gives you more than one way out. If you lose, remember the first decision that left you stuck and practice a different response in the next attempt. That gives you something specific to improve besides the final result.

Roblox’s badge description does not say what happens if you disconnect or change servers between wins. For a straightforward attempt, play both matches back to back in the same session and check the result after each one. See the [badge guide](/guides/badges/) if you want an alternate goal for a long match.

**Source reviewed November 9, 2026.** Badge conditions come from the linked Roblox records. The route suggestions are strategies to try, and this wiki has not tested streak behavior across servers.
