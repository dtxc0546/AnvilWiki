## How should a beginner plan the first team?

Begin with a question about the stage rather than a rarity label. In one attempt, observe where enemies first get past your setup. Then review whether you needed earlier coverage, a different placement or another upgrade. Changing everything at once makes that comparison harder.

| Decision | What to inspect in your current game | What to write down |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening placement | The visible path and legal placement area | Where your first unit can engage enemies |
| First purchase | The displayed deployment and upgrade costs | What you can afford now |
| Team change | The actual unit descriptions and stage needs | Which missing role you want to evaluate |
| Failed wave | Where the first leak occurred | One adjustment for the next attempt |

A more expensive unit is not automatically the right opening choice if it leaves you unable to cover the early part of an attempt. Conversely, adding another unit is not automatically better than improving one already placed. Compare the options displayed by the game and test one decision at a time; this site has no measured cost-efficiency ranking to substitute for that comparison.

1. Record the reward after a successful redemption.
2. Identify a specific gap in your current squad or progression.
3. Read the relevant purchase or reroll screen, including any displayed cost and result information.
4. Decide whether to spend now or keep the reward until the decision is clearer.
